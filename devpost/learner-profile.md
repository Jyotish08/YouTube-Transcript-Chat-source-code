# Learner Profile

## Initial Idea
"Make any video askable. You can Control-F a document, but you can't Control-F a video. I want to build a tool that lets anyone interrogate YouTube videos and uploaded recordings in plain language, where every answer cites the exact second it was spoken as a clickable deep link."

## Demonstrated Technical and Agent Experience
Familiar with modern TypeScript, React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Supabase PostgreSQL, and serverless Edge Functions (Deno). Experienced with AI coding workflows and multi-agent coordination.

## Planning-Workflow Experience
Familiar with spec-driven development, proof-of-concept boundaries, and flipped-interaction planning methodologies.

## Desired Learning Outcome
Mastering end-to-end flipped-interaction workflows: scoping a tight proof-of-concept, establishing non-technical product boundaries in a PRD, drafting unambiguous technical specifications, and managing external AI API orchestrations safely.

## Interests, Inspirations, and Goals
Passionate about knowledge retrieval, video chronometry, and making long-form educational lectures and hackathon submissions instantly auditable and verifiable.

## Vocabulary and Concepts Likely to Need Explanation
- Vector embeddings and cosine distance indexing (`pgvector` / `ivfflat`).
- Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) word-level timecode alignment.
- Mitigating IP blocks in edge environments (Bright Data dataset scraping vs. direct browser scraping).
- Hybrid retrieval mechanisms (combining semantic search with literal regex scans for exact mention counts).

## Collaboration and Communication Preferences
Concise, structured, and goal-oriented. Prefers clear architectural trade-offs and prescriptive specifications.

## Areas Where the Learner Wants Ownership
System architecture, security isolation (keeping credentials off the browser client), UI look-and-feel, and ensuring judges have a frictionless zero-key sandbox experience.

## Likely Support Needs
Structuring formal hackathon planning deliverables and ensuring complete alignment between scope, PRD, and technical specifications.
